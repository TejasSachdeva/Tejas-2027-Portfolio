Mini Precision Vise

Custom-Machined Mini Precision Vise

Project Overview

A functional mini vise built from aluminum and brass stock. Each component required a different combination of machines and operations. The build spanned manual milling, turning, CNC milling, knurling, broaching, and hand tapping.

Components & How They Were Made

Main Body
Milled from rectangular aluminum stock. End-mill for facing and the sliding channel; drilled for the brass insert and mounting holes.
Moving Block
Milled from aluminum stock with an end-mill, then drilled for the shaft-retention bushing and fastener holes.
Contact Plates (×2)
CNC milled from brass for flat, parallel jaw faces. Drilled and threaded for attachment screws.
Threaded Insert
Turned on the lathe, then hand-tapped 1/4-20 UNC on the lathe to keep the tap square to the bore.
Shaft-Retention Bushing
Turned on the lathe; seats in the moving block and captures the lead screw.
Shaft
Pre-threaded aluminum rod cut to length. One end machined square on the lathe to mate with the knob.
Knob
Turned and faced on the lathe, knurled for grip, then broached to a square bore to accept the square shaft end — a positive-drive coupling with no set screw.
